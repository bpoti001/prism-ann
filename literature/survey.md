# Verified Literature Survey: ReBAC Authorization & Filtered HNSW (`literature/survey.md`)

All 23 references below have been verified against upstream proceedings (USENIX ATC, ACM SIGMOD, PVLDB, WWW, IEEE TPAMI, Software: Practice and Experience, and arXiv) and maintain 1:1 parity with [`paper/prism_ann.tex`](file:///Users/tejap/memory_paper/prism_ann/paper/prism_ann.tex):

1. **Google Zanzibar (USENIX ATC 2019)** (`pang2019zanzibar`): R. Pang et al., *"Zanzibar: Google's Consistent, Global Authorization System,"* Proc. USENIX ATC 2019, pp. 33–46.
2. **HNSW (IEEE TPAMI 2020)** (`malkov2018hnsw`): Y. A. Malkov and D. A. Yashunin, *"Efficient and robust approximate nearest neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs,"* IEEE TPAMI, vol. 42, no. 4, pp. 824–836, 2020.
3. **FAISS (arXiv 2024)** (`douze2024faiss`): M. Douze et al., *"The Faiss library,"* `arXiv:2401.08281`, 2024.
4. **Filtered-DiskANN (WWW 2023)** (`gollapudi2023filtered`): S. Gollapudi et al., *"Filtered-DiskANN: Graph Algorithms for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search with Filters,"* Proc. ACM Web Conference (WWW), 2023, pp. 3406–3416.
5. **ACORN (SIGMOD 2024)** (`patel2024acorn`): L. Patel, P. Kraft, C. Guestrin, and M. Zaharia, *"ACORN: Performant and Predicate-Agnostic Search Over Vector Embeddings and Structured Data,"* Proc. ACM Manag. Data (SIGMOD), vol. 2, no. 3, 2024 (`arXiv:2403.04871`).
6. **HoneyBee (arXiv 2025)** (`zhong2025honeybee`): H. Zhong, M. Lentz, N. Narodytska, A. Szekeres, and K. Rong, *"HoneyBee: Efficient Role-based Access Control for Vector Databases via Dynamic Partitioning,"* `arXiv:2505.01538`, May 2025.
7. **Policy-Aware Vector Search (SeQureDB / SIGMOD 2026)** (`yalamarthi2026policy`): L. S. Yalamarthi and P. Pappachan, *"Policy-aware Vector Search: A Vision for Fine Grained Access Control in Vector Databases,"* SeQureDB Workshop (SIGMOD), `arXiv:2606.19803`, 2026.
8. **UNG — Unified Navigating Graph (SIGMOD 2024)** (`cai2024ung`): Y. Cai, J. Shi, Y. Chen, and W. Zheng, *"Navigating Labels and Vectors: A Unified Approach to Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search,"* Proc. ACM Manag. Data (SIGMOD), vol. 2, no. 6, 2024.
9. **SeRF (SIGMOD 2024)** (`zuo2024serf`): C. Zuo, M. Qiao, W. Zhou, F. Li, and D. Deng, *"SeRF: Segment Graph for Range-Filtering Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search,"* Proc. ACM Manag. Data (SIGMOD), vol. 2, no. 1, 2024.
10. **UNIFY / HSIG (VLDB 2024)** (`liang2024unify`): A. Liang, P. Zhang, B. Yao, Z. Chen, Y. Song, and G. Cheng, *"UNIFY: Unified Index for Range Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbors Search,"* PVLDB, vol. 17, no. 11, 2024.
11. **RoarGraph (VLDB 2024)** (`chen2024roargraph`): M. Chen, K. Zhang, Z. He, Y. Jing, and X. S. Wang, *"RoarGraph: A Projected Bipartite Graph for Efficient Cross-Modal Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search,"* PVLDB, vol. 17, no. 11, pp. 2735–2749, 2024.
12. **SIEVE (VLDB 2025)** (`li2025sieve`): Z. Li, S. Huang, W. Ding, Y. Park, and J. Chen, *"SIEVE: Effective Filtered Vector Search with Collection of Indexes,"* PVLDB, vol. 18, 2025.
13. **BEIR (NeurIPS 2021)** (`thakur2021beir`): N. Thakur, N. Reimers, A. Rücklé, A. Srivastava, and I. Gurevych, *"BEIR: A Heterogeneous Benchmark for Zero-shot Evaluation of Information Retrieval Models,"* NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks, 2021.
14. **Sentence-BERT (EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019)** (`reimers2019sentencebert`): N. Reimers and I. Gurevych, *"Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks,"* Proc. EMNLP-IJCNLP, pp. 3982–3992, 2019.
15. **FANNBench (arXiv 2025)** (`iff2025fannbench`): P. Iff, P. Brügger, M. Chrapek, D. Kochergin, M. Besta, and T. Hoefler, *"Benchmarking Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Algorithms on Transformer-based Embedding Vectors,"* ETH Zurich, `arXiv:2507.21989`, 2025. (Benchmark framework and dataset evaluation for filtered ANNS under selectivity/correlation stress.)
16. **Global-Local Selectivity Metric (arXiv 2026)** (`amanbayev2026gls`): A. Amanbayev et al., *"Measuring and Mitigating Correlation Bias in Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search via Global-Local Selectivity,"* `arXiv:2602.11443`, Feb. 2026. (Proposes the Global-Local Selectivity ratio $r = S_{\text{loc}}/S$ characterizing query-filter spatial depletion/enrichment.)
17. **Curator (arXiv 2026)** (`curator2026`): Y. Jin, Y. Wu, W. Hu, B. M. Maggs, J. Yang, X. Zhang, and D. Zhuo, *"Curator: Efficient Vector Search with Low-Selectivity Filters,"* `arXiv:2601.01291`, 2026. (Low-selectivity dual-index / shared-graph hierarchical routing trees.)
18. **Veda / EffVeda (arXiv 2026)** (`veda2026`): S. Chinta et al., *"Veda & EffVeda: Access-Aware Vector Indexing for Low-Selectivity Fine-Grained Retrieval,"* `arXiv:2605.01342`, May 2026. (Access-aware vector partitioning and dynamic index maintenance for RBAC/attribute policies, contrasted against Prism-ANN's non-monotonic Zanzibar DAG cut and Z-order interval scanner.)
19. **RACORN (arXiv Nov. 2026)** (`kim2026racorn`): Y. Kim and G. Choe, *"RACORN: Robust Predicate-Agnostic Filtered Vector Search under Extreme Selectivity,"* `arXiv:2607.00768v2` ([official code](https://github.com/naver/racorn)), Nov. 2026. (Adaptive fallback traversal through rejected neighbors over external predicate maps.)
20. **EMA (arXiv Nov. 2026)** (`li2026ema`): M. Li, B. Lu, J. Cheng, and C. Ma, *"EMA: Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search with General Attribute Filtering and Dynamic Updates,"* `arXiv:2606.00734v2`, Nov. 2026.
21. **AnalyticDB-V (VLDB 2020)** (`wei2020analyticdbv`): C. Wei et al., *"AnalyticDB-V: A Hybrid Analytical Engine Towards Query Fusion for Structured and Unstructured Data,"* PVLDB, vol. 13, no. 12, pp. 3152–3165, 2020.
22. **Milvus (SIGMOD 2021)** (`wang2021milvus`): J. Wang et al., *"Milvus: A Purpose-Built Vector Data Management System,"* Proc. ACM SIGMOD, pp. 2614–2627, 2021.
23. **EngramRAG (arXiv 2026)** (`potineni2026engramrag`): B. Potineni, L. Giri, A. Jain, V. Kutsyy, and R. Pentakota, *"EngramRAG: Dynamic Usage-Weighted Topology and Synaptic Consolidation for Multi-Hop Agentic Memory,"* `arXiv:2609.32049`, 2026.
