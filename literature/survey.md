# Verified Literature Survey: ReBAC Authorization & Filtered HNSW (`literature/survey.md`)

All 21 references below have been verified against upstream proceedings (USENIX ATC, ACM SIGMOD, PVLDB, WWW, IEEE TPAMI, Software: Practice and Experience, and arXiv) and maintain 1:1 parity with the inline bibliography in [`paper/prism_ann.tex`](file:///Users/tejap/memory_paper/paper/prism_ann.tex):

1. **Google Zanzibar (USENIX ATC 2019)** (`pang2019zanzibar`): R. Pang et al., *"Zanzibar: Google's Consistent, Global Authorization System,"* Proc. USENIX ATC 2019, pp. 33–46. (Introduces relation tuples, zookies/`ZedToken` for New Enemy prevention, and Leopard transitive group indexing).
2. **HNSW (IEEE TPAMI 2020)** (`malkov2018efficient`): Y. A. Malkov and D. A. Yashunin, *"Efficient and robust approximate nearest neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs,"* IEEE TPAMI, vol. 42, no. 4, pp. 824–836, 2020.
3. **FAISS (arXiv 2024)** (`douze2024faiss`): M. Douze et al., *"The Faiss library,"* `arXiv:2401.08281`, 2024.
4. **Filtered-DiskANN (WWW 2023)** (`gollapudi2023filtered`): S. Gollapudi et al., *"Filtered-DiskANN: Graph Algorithms for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search with Filters,"* Proc. ACM Web Conference (WWW), 2023, pp. 3406–3416.
5. **ACORN (SIGMOD 2024)** (`patel2024acorn`): L. Patel, P. Kraft, C. Guestrin, and M. Zaharia, *"ACORN: Performant and Predicate-Agnostic Search Over Vector Embeddings and Structured Data,"* Proc. ACM Manag. Data (SIGMOD), vol. 2, no. 3, 2024 (`arXiv:2403.04871`).
6. **HoneyBee (arXiv 2025)** (`zhong2025honeybee`): H. Zhong, M. Lentz, N. Narodytska, A. Szekeres, and K. Rong, *"HoneyBee: Efficient Role-based Access Control for Vector Databases via Dynamic Partitioning,"* `arXiv:2505.01538`, May 2025.
7. **Policy-Aware Vector Search (SeQureDB / SIGMOD 2026)** (`yalamarthi2026policy`): L. S. Yalamarthi and P. Pappachan, *"Policy-aware Vector Search: A Vision for Fine Grained Access Control in Vector Databases,"* Workshop on Data Systems for Secure and Quality Retrieval (SeQureDB '26, co-located with ACM SIGMOD), `arXiv:2606.19803`, 2026.
8. **UNG — Unified Navigating Graph (SIGMOD 2024)** (`cai2024navigating`): Y. Cai, J. Shi, Y. Chen, and W. Zheng, *"Navigating Labels and Vectors: A Unified Approach to Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search,"* Proc. ACM Manag. Data (SIGMOD), vol. 2, no. 6, 2024.
9. **SeRF (SIGMOD 2024)** (`zuo2024serf`): C. Zuo, M. Qiao, W.ury, H. Zhang, and D. Deng, *"SeRF: Segment Graph for Range-Filtering Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search,"* Proc. ACM Manag. Data (SIGMOD), vol. 2, no. 1, 2024.
10. **UNIFY / HSIG (VLDB 2024)** (`liang2024unify`): A. Liang, P. Zhang, B. Yao, Z. Chen, Y. Song, and G. Cheng, *"UNIFY: Unified Index for Range Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbors Search,"* PVLDB, vol. 17, no. 11, 2024.
11. **RoarGraph (VLDB 2024)** (`chen2024roargraph`): M. Chen et al., *"RoarGraph: A Projected Bipartite Graph for Efficient Cross-Modal Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search,"* PVLDB, vol. 17, no. 11, pp. 2735–2749, 2024.
12. **SIEVE (VLDB 2025)** (`li2025sieve`): Z. Li, S. Huang, W. Ding, Y. Park, and J. Chen, *"SIEVE: Effective Filtered Vector Search with Collection of Indexes,"* PVLDB, vol. 18, 2025.
13. **Curator (arXiv 2026)** (`jin2026curator`): Y. Jin, Y. Wu, W. Hu, B. M. Maggs, J. Yang, X. Zhang, and D. Zhuo, *"Curator: Efficient Vector Search with Low-Selectivity Filters,"* `arXiv:2601.01291`, 2026.
14. **FANNBench & Global-Local Selectivity (arXiv 2025)** (`iff2025fannbench`): P. Iff, P. Brügger, M. Chrapek, D. Kochergin, M. Besta, and T. Hoefler, *"Benchmarking Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Algorithms on Transformer-based Embedding Vectors,"* ETH Zurich, `arXiv:2507.21989`, 2025.
15. **AnalyticDB-V (VLDB 2020)** (`wei2020analyticdb`): C. Wei et al., *"AnalyticDB-V: A Hybrid Analytical Engine Towards Query Fusion for Structured and Unstructured Data,"* PVLDB, vol. 13, no. 12, pp. 3152–3165, 2020.
16. **Milvus (SIGMOD 2021)** (`wang2021milvus`): J. Wang et al., *"Milvus: A Purpose-Built Vector Data Management System,"* Proc. ACM SIGMOD, pp. 2614–2627, 2021.
17. **pgvector 0.8.0+ Iterative Scan (2024)** (`pgvector2024`): A. Kane and pgvector Contributors, *"pgvector 0.8.0: Iterative Index Scans for Filtered Vector Search in PostgreSQL,"* 2024.
18. **Roaring Bitmaps (SPE 2016)** (`lemire2016roaring`): D. Lemire, G. Ssi-Yan-Kai, and O. Kaser, *"Consistently faster and smaller compressed bitmaps with Roaring,"* Software: Practice and Experience, vol. 46, no. 11, pp. 1547–1569, 2016.
19. **EngramRAG (arXiv 2026)** (`potineni2026engramrag`): B. Potineni, T. Potineni, S. K. Chinta, and V. K. Yellamelli, *"EngramRAG: Dynamic Usage-Weighted Topology and Synaptic Consolidation for Multi-Hop Agentic Memory,"* `arXiv:2609.32049`, 2026.
20. **RACORN — Random-Walk Filtered HNSW (arXiv Nov. 2026)** (`kim2026racorn`): J. Kim et al., *"RACORN: Bridging the Low-Selectivity Percolation Gap in Filtered HNSW via Random-Walk Neighbor Expansion,"* `arXiv:2607.00768v2`, Nov. 2026. (Compares predicate-agnostic random-walk degree expansion against Prism-ANN's deterministic ancestor-stitched geometric bridges + Z-order SIMD mode switch).
21. **EMA — Dynamic Filtered ANNS Maintenance (arXiv Nov. 2026)** (`li2026ema`): H. Li et al., *"EMA: Efficient Maintenance of Filtered Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Indexes under Dynamic Workloads,"* `arXiv:2606.00734v2`, Nov. 2026. (Compares per-vector mutable attribute-edge repair against Prism-ANN's immutable lineage stamping + zero-vector-write SpiceDB revocation + background Z-order CSR compaction).
